# Nine Lives

See what you're wearing. See what they're wearing. Match if you want to.

Nine Lives is a low-friction wardrobe and outfit app built around the way people actually deal with clothes.

## The idea

Adding clothes should not feel like cataloging inventory. Take photos or videos haphazardly — clothes on the bed, hanging up, mirror photos, individual pieces, whatever — and let Nine Lives turn that into a usable closet.

From there, pick an outfit yourself, randomize one, save combinations you like, or compare what you're wearing with someone else.

## Core experience

- **Closet** — quickly capture and organize clothes without requiring meticulous manual entry.
- **Outfits** — combine pieces, save outfits, and shuffle/randomize.
- **Lock + reroll** — keep pieces you like and randomize the rest.
- **Together** — compare two people's outfits side by side.
- **Match if you want to** — coordinate colors or overall feel without requiring identical outfits.
- **Shared choices** — compare candidate outfits when deciding what to wear together.

## Product principle

**The app should do the organizing work.**

The useful part of Nine Lives starts before someone has a perfectly cataloged wardrobe. Capture should be forgiving, organization should be lightweight, and outfit selection should be fast.

## Initial MVP

1. Add clothing from photos.
2. Maintain a personal closet.
3. Build an outfit manually.
4. Randomize an outfit.
5. Lock an item and reroll the rest.
6. Save outfits.
7. Connect with one other person.
8. Compare outfits side by side.

Video ingestion, automatic garment extraction, deeper matching intelligence, weather, wear history, packing, laundry state, and other recommendation features can follow after the basic loop works well.
